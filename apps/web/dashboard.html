The API

POST  /api/flow/chat/completions

OpenAI-compatible, streaming. model is provider/model-id — e.g. groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile, openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-r1:free, google/gemini-2.5-flash, mistral/mistral-large-latest — or one of your aliases. Your cookie is the credential; nothing else authenticates here.
