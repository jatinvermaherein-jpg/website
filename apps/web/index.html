The two engines

Flow

The gateway. Use-it-or-lose-it scheduling over every key you own: the quota that dies first is spent first, failures ride a flagged ladder, and every number on screen is measured. Per account, per key, your own limits.

Crew

The court. Identical agents, different seats — Lead, Builders, Auditors, Scribe, Scout — over one immutable stream. Verdicts need a falsification attempt; unproven is a real answer; a dead seat’s work resumes from the bench.

A crew can run on Flow, on its own keys, or on its own system — your choice, per project. The stream is the record; everything else is a mirror.
